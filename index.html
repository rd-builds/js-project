About Sevya Foundation

At Sevya Foundation, nurturing children means nurturing the promise of India. Since our inception, we have been driven by a mission to transform childhood landscapes across the country through compassion, care, and consistent efforts. We remain steadfast in our resolve to help children build a secure childhood, laying the foundation for a secure future.

Our approach at Sevya Foundation is holistic, addressing multiple aspects of a child’s well-being — access to health and nutrition, quality education, protection from harm, psychosocial support, and overall development. By safeguarding children from exploitation and ensuring equal opportunities, we empower them to thrive as the architects of tomorrow’s nation.

We pledge to make every impact meaningful and comprehensive for India’s children. However, shaping young lives cannot happen in isolation. It requires a unified effort from communities, government bodies, and supporters. By collaborating with grassroots workers, policymakers, donors, and partners, Sevya Foundation works to build an enabling ecosystem where children can truly flourish.

With the help of volunteers and partners, we continue to expand our reach and impact more lives every day.

Shiksha Ki Raksha, Bhavishya Ki Raksha!

From early childhood to adolescence, Sevya Foundation is committed to empowering underserved children through education, nutrition, healthcare, and community support initiatives. We believe every child deserves a safe learning environment, equal opportunities, and the confidence to dream bigger. Through inclusive programs, volunteer-driven campaigns, and partnerships across communities, we work to remove barriers that prevent children from accessing quality education and essential resources. From supporting rural schools to organizing food and awareness drives, Sevya Foundation continues to create meaningful impact and brighter futures for children across India.
